Comprehensive Scope & Foundations

Project Scope & Problem Analysis

Addressing the systemic agricultural, ecological, and economic bottlenecks in Sri Lanka's high-value "Queen of Spices" cultivation.

⚠️

The Agricultural Problem

Small- and medium-scale cardamom farmers in Kandy, Badulla, and Matale depend on cardamom as their primary livelihood. Presently, they rely strictly on manual observation and personal experience:

  • Devastating Yield Reductions: Leaf blight and leaf spot spread rapidly in humid mountain microclimates, leading to 25%–40% seasonal harvest destruction.
  • Premature Harvesting & Borer Attacks: Capsule borers leave microscopic punctures that ruin batches. Farmers harvest immature green pods due to lack of objective maturity markers.
  • Subjective Quality Grading: Farmers sell unsorted or subjectively graded pods, receiving low baseline prices while export traders reap premium margins.
  • Agrochemical Overuse & Soil Degradation: Delayed manual detection leads to frantic over-application of chemicals, threatening the delicate Knuckles Forest ecosystem.
🌱

The Cardamo Solution

Cardamo introduces an integrated, machine learning–based mobile decision support framework tailored for real-field conditions:

  • Multi-Disease Vision Diagnostics: Rapid identification of 5 prevalent local foliar diseases trained on field-captured highland datasets.
  • YOLO Pod & Pest Localization: Real-time detection of capsule borer holes and multi-factor maturity scoring (color, size, geometry).
  • DOA & EDB Standard Grading: Automated RGB/HSV colorimetric analysis, pod diameter calculation, and batch-mode quality certification.
  • Actionable Harvest-Scale Market Intelligence: Price forecasting aligned with the farmer’s specific harvest weight (1 kg, 5 kg, 50 kg+) and auction trends.

Identified Academic & Field Research Gaps

01

Limited Local Disease Scope

Existing studies evaluate only 2 or 3 diseases under laboratory settings, completely overlooking chlorotic streak and vein clearing found in Sri Lankan highland climates.

02

Controlled Datasets vs Real Plantations

Most prior vision models were tested on sterile lab datasets rather than field images captured with variable natural lighting, mist, and leaf moisture.

03

Absence of Sri Lankan Pod Grading Standards

International research uses overseas criteria that do not correlate with Sri Lanka Department of Export Agriculture (DOA) and Export Development Board (EDB) grades.

04

Manual Capsule Borer & Maturity Checking

Maturity identification is restricted to static color charts, while destructive capsule borer identification depends entirely on naked-eye field checks.

05

Scarcity of Spice Market Predictive Models

While rice and tea enjoy extensive econometric modeling, cardamom farmers receive no harvest-quantity-based price forecast or selling advisory.

06

Unintegrated Disconnected Tools

Existing tools fail to unify leaf pathology, harvest quality, and market economics into a single coherent farmer workflow.

Academic Literature Survey & Grounding

[1] U. Jayasinghe (HARTI - 2016)

Cardamom Cultivation in Knuckles Conservation Forest: Documents 25–40% yield losses caused by pests, diseases, and environmental degradation in Sri Lankan highland cardamom plantations.

Foundation for loss-reduction justification and central highland scope.
[2] C.K. Sunil and C. D. Jaidhar (2021)

Cardamom Plant Disease Detection Approach Using EfficientNetV2: Validates >90% accuracy for image-based leaf disease detection, applicable to leaf blight and leaf spot.

Baseline for deep convolutional architecture comparison in leaf pathology.
[3] M. Murugan (2021)

Effects of Cardamom Cultivation on Its Agroecosystem: Confirms humidity-driven pest outbreaks such as thrips and shoot borers, highlighting soil degradation in humid regions.

Justifies capsule borer detection and automated spray advisories.
[4] W. M. M. Weerakoon (2017)

Impact of Cardamom Cultivation on Soil Seed Banks: Shows long-term biodiversity loss and decline in soil health caused by unsustainable cultivation practices in the Knuckles region.

Highlights need for accurate diagnosis before chemical intervention.
[5] Export Development Board Sri Lanka (EDB - 2019)

Spices & Allied Products Sector Capability Report: Provides national cardamom production statistics (5,000–6,000 MT across 3,000 ha) and emphasizes export quality challenges.

Standard reference for pod grading metrics and export market analysis.
Main Research Objective

Integrated Machine Learning Decision Support System

"To design and implement an integrated machine learning–based intelligent decision support system that enhances cardamom farming and marketing by enabling early disease detection, objective quality grading, market prediction and analysis, and efficient pest infestation control, thereby improving productivity, export quality consistency, and farmer income."

Engineering Process

Methodology & System Architecture

From plantation ground-truthing to edge inference: a rigorous 4-phase research methodology delivering production-grade machine learning.

1

Phase 1: Field Data Collection & Domain Validation

Field expeditions across Kandy, Badulla, and Matale cardamom plantations. Supervised ground-truth labeling by retired Agriculture Department officer Mrs. Pavithra Seniwirathna and integration of historical DOA archives.

2

Phase 2: Preprocessing & Vision Model Training

Dataset augmentation, illumination normalization, and training state-of-the-art vision models: YOLOv10, YOLOv8, and EfficientNetV2 with transfer learning for leaf pathology and pod grading.

3

Phase 3: System Integration & Mobile Pipeline

Designing a farmer-friendly mobile client backed by a lightweight RESTful inference engine. Integrating leaf diagnostics, pod defect analysis, and market forecast algorithms into a single unified schema.

4

Phase 4: Field Testing & Farmer Cooperative Trials

Field validation in collaboration with HARTI-linked agricultural cooperatives. Benchmarking accuracy against agricultural officer diagnoses, testing low-light conditions, and gathering System Usability Scale (SUS) metrics.

Module 2: Smart Pod Monitoring & Caring System

Researcher: Dissanayake K.S.S. • Reg: IT22223180

✓ Capsule Borer Localization: Locates sub-millimeter borer entry points and surface necrotic scarring utilizing real-time YOLO detectors.
✓ Harvest Timing Advisory: Prevents premature green picking which accounts for up to 30% loss of dry weight and volatile oil potency.

Maturity Verification

Calibrated against the Department of Export Agriculture maturity index (capsule firmness, ribbed curvature, seed coloration).

Module 4: Cardamom Pod Quality Grading

Researcher: Kulasooriya K.S.D • Reg: IT22158840
✓ RGB & HSV Colorimetry: Quantifies deep green, pale green, yellowish, and brownish pods with pixel-level color segmentation.
✓ Geometric Calibration: Calculates pod length, transverse diameter, and aspect ratio to separate Grade 1 bold pods from smaller capsules.
✓ YOLOv10 Surface Defect Detection: Identifies thrips scars, epidermal splits, and mechanical bruises.

YOLOv10 HSV Color Space DOA & EDB Standards OpenCV Contours
Export Grade Alignment

Enforces the official Sri Lanka standards: Ceylon Cardamom Green (Special/Regular) based on capsule diameter (>7mm, 6-7mm) and defect allowances.
